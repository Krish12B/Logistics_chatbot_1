### 2. Air Freight Cargo Limits, ULD Containers and Volumetric Rules
Air freight cargo must comply with International Air Transport Association (IATA) aircraft weight and balance limits.

- **Passenger Aircraft Lower Deck Restrictions:**
  - Maximum height per package: 160 cm (63 inches).
  - Maximum package weight for non-palletized cargo: 150 kg (330 lbs) per piece unless pre-arranged heavy-cargo loaders are deployed.
  - Standard Lower Deck ULDs:
    - **LD3 (AKE):** Base dimensions 156 cm × 153 cm (61.5" × 60.4"), height 163 cm (64"). Max gross weight: 1,588 kg (3,500 lbs). Internal volume: 4.3 m³ (152 cu ft).
    - **LD7 / PMC Pallet:** Base 318 cm × 244 cm (125" × 96"). Max gross weight: 6,804 kg (15,000 lbs).

- **Main Deck Dedicated Freighter Cargo Limits:**
  - Maximum cargo height: Up to 300 cm (118 inches) for main deck Boeing 747-8F and 777F freighters.
  - Single piece maximum weight: Up to 25,000 kg (55,000 lbs) subject to floor load limits (typically max 1,000 kg/m² or 200 lbs/sq ft without load spreaders).

- **Volumetric Weight (Dimensional Weight) Calculation:**
  Air freight charges are based on the greater of actual gross weight or volumetric weight.
  - Metric Formula: Volumetric Weight (kg) = [Length (cm) × Width (cm) × Height (cm)] / 6000
  - Imperial Formula: Volumetric Weight (lbs) = [Length (in) × Width (in) × Height (in)] / 139
  - IATA Standard Conversion: 1 cubic meter (cbm) = 167 kg for air freight.

### 3. Pallet Dimensions, Stacking Standards and Payload Capacities
FleetHarbor standardizes on internationally certified wooden and composite pallets treated in accordance with ISPM 15 standards:

- **Euro Pallet (EUR-1 / EPAL):**
  - Dimensions: 1,200 mm × 800 mm × 144 mm (47.2" × 31.5" × 5.7").
  - Tare Weight: 25 kg (55 lbs).
  - Maximum Safe Working Payload: 1,500 kg (3,307 lbs) dynamic load, 4,000 kg (8,818 lbs) static load.
  - Container Loading Capacity: A 20ft container accommodates 11 Euro pallets; a 40ft container accommodates 24 to 25 Euro pallets.
  - Recommended Maximum Stack Height: 180 cm (including pallet base) for standard warehousing.

- **Standard Industrial Pallet (ISO / US GMA Standard):**
  - Dimensions: 1,200 mm × 1,000 mm × 162 mm (48" × 40" × 6.4").
  - Tare Weight: ~22 to 28 kg.
  - Maximum Safe Dynamic Working Load: 1,500 kg (3,307 lbs).
  - Container Loading Capacity: A 20ft container accommodates 10 ISO pallets; a 40ft container accommodates 20 to 21 ISO pallets.

### 4. Over-The-Road (OTR) Trucking and Road Freight Limits
Road freight haulage operates under Federal Highway Administration (FHWA) and state Department of Transportation regulations:

- **Standard 53ft Dry Van Truckload Limits:**
  - Exterior Dimensions: Length 53ft (16.15 m), Width 102 in (2.6 m), Height 13ft 6in (4.11 m).
  - Interior Usable Dimensions: Length 52ft 6in, Width 99 in, Height 110 in.
  - Usable Cargo Volume: ~3,800 cubic feet (107.6 m³).
  - Maximum Legal Cargo Weight: 44,000 to 45,000 lbs (19,958 to 20,412 kg).

- **Legal Axle Weight Limits (US Interstate Standard):**
  - Steer Axle: Maximum 12,000 lbs (5,443 kg).
  - Drive Tandem Axles: Maximum 34,000 lbs (15,422 kg).
  - Trailer Tandem Axles: Maximum 34,000 lbs (15,422 kg).
  - Maximum Total Gross Vehicle Weight (GVW): 80,000 lbs (36,287 kg) without heavy-haul oversized permits.

- **Oversize and Overweight (OOG / Heavy Haul) Requirements:**
  - Oversize loads exceeding 102 inches in width, 13 feet 6 inches in height, or 80,000 lbs gross weight require state permits, escort pilot vehicles, and daylight transit restrictions.

### 5. Express Courier and Small Parcel Dimensional Restrictions
FleetHarbor Express parcel division enforces the following threshold limits for small package routing:

- Maximum weight per single package: 70 kg (150 lbs).
- Maximum package length: 274 cm (108 inches).
- Maximum length plus girth: Length + 2 × (Width + Height) cannot exceed 400 cm (157 inches).
- Packages weighing above 30 kg (66 lbs) or exceeding 120 cm on any single dimension incur Special Handling Surcharges (SHS) and mandatory team-lift labels.
