# 📋 FOB vs CIF Shipping Terms (Incoterms 2020)

### 1. Overview
FOB and CIF are standard International Commercial Terms (Incoterms) defining the division of transport costs, marine insurance, and risk of loss between buyers and sellers.

### 2. Key Differences
| Feature | FOB (Free On Board) | CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight) |
|---|---|---|
| **Seller Responsibility** | Delivers cargo safely loaded on board the vessel at the origin port. Clears export customs. | Pays for inland transport, export customs, ocean freight carriage, and minimum marine insurance to the destination port. |
| **Buyer Responsibility** | Pays ocean freight carriage, marine cargo insurance, destination terminal fees, import duties, and inland delivery. | Pays destination port handling charges, import customs clearance, tariffs, and inland transport to final facility. |
| **Risk Transfer Point** | Transfers to buyer when cargo is loaded on board the vessel at the port of origin. | Transfers to buyer when cargo is loaded on board the vessel at origin (even though seller pays freight & insurance). |
| **Applicable Transport** | Maritime ocean and inland waterway transport only. | Maritime ocean and inland waterway transport only. |

The FleetHarbor end-to-end logistics lifecycle encompasses six audited, sequential milestones connecting booking, multimodal transit, regulatory clearance, and final physical delivery.

### 2. Six Operational Lifecycle Stages
