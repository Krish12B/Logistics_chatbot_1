## 1. Executive Summary & Workforce Architecture
FleetHarbor Logistics operates an international workforce of 500 certified logistics professionals deployed across 15 global mega-hubs and regional gateways. Operations span 24/7/365 across three primary shift rotations: Day Shift (06:00 - 14:30), Swing Shift (14:00 - 22:30), and Overnight Shift (22:00 - 06:30), supported by an On-Call Escalation rotation.
