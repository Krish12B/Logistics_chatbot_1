QUESTION: What causes shipment delays?
----------------------------------------------------------------------
KNOWLEDGE BASE:
FAQ (delay): Shipments can be delayed by port congestion, bad weather, customs holds, or missing documents. When a delay is logged, the status changes to 'Delayed' and the delay reason is shown on the shipment record. The estimated delivery date is updated once the delay is confirmed.
